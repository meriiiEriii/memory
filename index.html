Four steps to a brighter past

Restoring a photo used to mean layers, masks and expensive software. Memory Lane boils it down to a couple of clicks.

01

Upload your photo

Drop in a scan or an old family picture from your device. Large photos are handled gracefully, and nothing ever leaves your computer.

02

Let Auto-Fix restore it

One click reads the photo and fixes what it finds: black & white prints are colourised, soft focus is sharpened, grain is smoothed and faded tone is rebalanced.

03

Slide and compare

Drag the handle across the photo to slide between the original and the restored version, then fine-tune anything with honest, simple sliders.

04

Erase unwanted guests

Brush over a stray relative, a stain or a scribble and remove it. The fill studies the photo and rebuilds the scene behind it — tones, textures and grain — so it looks like the object was never there.

Everything a memory needs

Small, focused tools that respect the photo — and the people in it.

Colourise black & white

Old monochrome prints get sensible, natural colour — warm skin, soft skies, believable greens — with the original brightness untouched.

Rescue blurry photos

Soft, out-of-focus prints are measured, then the blur itself is reversed with Richardson–Lucy deconvolution — rebuilding edges and detail instead of just sharpening what's left.

Slide-to-compare

A draggable handle splits the photo so you can sweep between the original and the restored version — and hold the eye button to peek at the past.

One-click Auto-Fix

Reads the photo's histogram, finds the crushed blacks and muddy whites, and rebalances exposure, contrast and colour automatically.

Remove objects for good

Paint over anything you do not want in the picture and an AI-style fill studies the surroundings, then rebuilds the scene behind it so the edit is invisible.

Full tonal control

Exposure, brightness, contrast, shadows, highlights, saturation and warmth — plus grain smoothing and edge sharpening for old prints.

Private by design

Every pixel is processed in your browser with canvas technology. No servers, no uploads — your family album stays yours.
